# ST205: Sample Surveys and Experiments

This folder contains the group coursework for **ST205: Sample Surveys and Experiments** (LSE).  
The project counted for **20% of the final course mark** and we achieved a score of **84/100**.

---

## 📌 Coursework Overview

**Dataset:** `collegedata.csv` (N = 1372 colleges across the United States)  
**Variables included:** institution name, state, region, type (public/private), HBCU status, demographic proportions (e.g. women, Black undergraduates), tuition (in-state, out-of-state), net price, faculty salaries, and more.  

**Key tasks:**
1. **Simple Random Sampling (SRS):**  
   - Selected a sample of 20 colleges with documented random numbers.  
   - Estimated means of in-state vs out-of-state tuition, conducted hypothesis test, and interpreted results.  
   - Estimated the proportion of Black undergraduates, with SE and 95% CI.

2. **Alternative Sampling Designs:**  
   - Discussed **stratified** vs **cluster** sampling: pros/cons, allocation methods, and efficiency.  
   - Implemented **stratified sampling** (by region), selected 20 colleges, and estimated mean in-state tuition.

3. **Systematic Sampling:**  
   - Selected a systematic sample of 20 colleges.  
   - Estimated average monthly faculty salary and provided SE.

4. **Ratio Estimation:**  
   - Estimated ratio of in-state to out-of-state tuition fees under SRS, with SE and 95% CI.

5. **Comparisons Across Designs:**  
   - Compared SRS, stratified, and systematic sampling methods in terms of precision, bias, and practicality.  
   - Evaluated trade-offs between bias, variance, and ease of implementation.

---

## 📊 Key Outcomes
- Produced **point estimates, standard errors, and confidence intervals** for tuition, faculty salaries, and demographic proportions.  
- Demonstrated use of **SRS, stratified, and systematic sampling**, including allocation strategies.  
- Compared efficiency of designs, highlighting when stratified designs yield gains in precision.  
- Applied ratio estimation to provide interpretable tuition comparisons.
